# Ruleset: ste

For documents the reader consults.

Adapted from ASD-STE100. The approved dictionary is not used, so the result is not conformant. Never describe it as conformant.

## Mechanical

- Cap instructions at 20 words and descriptive sentences at 25 words. Split the sentence, do not compress it.
- Write one instruction per sentence.
- Cap procedural paragraphs at six sentences.
- Convert passive to active and name the actor. Keep passive where the actor is genuinely unknown.
- Convert to simple present, simple past, or simple future.
- Restore dropped articles.
- Remove `-ing` forms used as verbs or adjectives.
- Put a sequence of steps in a vertical list.
- Move a warning or caution before the step it guards.
- Cut filler: "in order to", "due to the fact that", "it is important to note that".
- Collapse stacked hedging: "could potentially possibly" becomes "may".
- Replace fancy synonyms: utilize, leverage, facilitate, numerous, in the event that.
- Cut adverbs propping up weak verbs. Use a stronger verb or the number.

## Judgement

- Noun clusters longer than three words. Propose a break with a preposition or a verb.
- One term, one meaning. If the project defines a glossary, read it first and use its terms. Report synonym cycling with both terms and ask which one wins.
- Sentences that name a feeling instead of a mechanism. Propose the concrete fact, number, or instruction.
- Significance inflation: "pivotal", "testament to", "evolving landscape".
- Promotional language: "robust", "seamless", "powerful", "elegant".
- Vague attribution: "experts suggest", "it is widely held".
- Formulaic contrast: "despite the challenges, X continues to".
- Negative parallelism: "not just X, but Y".
- Ideas forced into groups of three.
- False ranges, where the two endpoints share no scale.
- Generic conclusions: "the future looks bright".
- Abstract metaphor nouns: substrate, vector, nexus, bedrock, surface, primitive as a noun.

## Not in this ruleset

Nothing here bans a plainly stated tradeoff. A document that hides a cost is a defect. Report hedging, not honesty.
